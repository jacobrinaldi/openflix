Openflix

What is Openflix

Openflix is an open source media streaming site like netflix, peacock and prime video, but it contains entirely public domain films meaning you could make a video with content from these without any copyright issues. This site doesn't need any login or any of your info either so just relex and enjoy some movies and TV.

How does Openflix work

Openflix in its self is just a link host to archive sites or other sites that host public domain works.

Why use Openflix

As stated previously Openflix is mostly a link host... So why use it instead of just looking them up? The answer is simple, Openflix makes the search less difficult by providing you a free library to search through easily.
